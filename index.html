02 — Selected work

Infrastructure projects

A few pieces of hands-on work that shaped how I approach infrastructure — connectivity, identity at scale, and endpoint security.

Company-wide network overhaul: third-party to Cisco Meraki

Challenge

The organization's network ran on a mix of third-party TP-Link and D-Link switches and access points, with no centralized visibility, inconsistent performance, and rising support overhead across sites.

Action

Planned and led a full migration of the company-wide network to Cisco Meraki, replacing over 21 switches and 34 access points and rebuilding network monitoring around the Meraki dashboard.

Result

A single-pane-of-glass, cloud-managed network with far greater visibility, consistency, and reliability across the entire organization.

Extending network connectivity across remote farm sites

Challenge

Multiple farm locations needed reliable connectivity beyond the reach of existing wired infrastructure.

Action

Deployed access points, point-to-point links, and wireless radio bridges, then monitored the network via the Cisco Meraki dashboard.

Result

Extended stable connectivity across sites and cut recurring connectivity faults.

Biometric attendance integration for 3,000+ employees

Challenge

A large distributed workforce needed reliable attendance capture feeding directly into HR and payroll.

Action

Administered the UTime Master biometric system and installed and maintained face and fingerprint devices across sites.

Result

Consistent, reliable clocking data integrated with payroll at scale.

Consolidating endpoint security & provisioning

Challenge

A 24/7 organization needed consistent endpoint protection and faster device provisioning across departments.

Action

Rolled out fleet-wide policies via the Sophos Admin Console and standardized provisioning through Microsoft Intune and SCCM, then automated recurring reporting workflows.

Result

Fewer manual setup steps and clearer visibility into device compliance.
